Step-by-Step Registration Guide

Follow these 4 steps to successfully submit your application on the SSP portal

1

Aadhaar-Based Registration

Visit scholarship.odisha.gov.in, click on "New Registration" and enter your Aadhaar number. Verify your identity using the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

2

Complete Your Profile Dashboard

Fill in your Academic details (college, course, year), Personal information, and Bank account details carefully. Ensure your bank account is active for DBT credit.

3

Select Eligible Scheme & Upload Documents

The portal auto-detects schemes you are eligible for based on your profile. Click "Apply" on the relevant scheme and upload all scanned documents in the required format (JPG/PDF, under 200KB).

4

Final Submit & Take Printout

Review your entire application carefully. Click "Final Submit" — this step cannot be undone. Download the acknowledgement slip and submit a physical copy to your institution's scholarship cell.
